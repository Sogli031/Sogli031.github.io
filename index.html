Rad

Rad is a personal Android app for tracking work shifts, overtime, vacation and sick leave, and for checking monthly payslips against the recorded shifts.

With the user's permission, the app finds payslip e-mails in the user's Gmail account and reads the attached PDF payslip, so the user does not have to download and import it manually. The app can also back up its own data to a hidden app folder in the user's Google Drive.
