Rad – Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Data the app accesses

  • Gmail (read-only): the app searches the user's mailbox only for payslip e-mails (subject "Obracun zarada") and downloads their PDF attachments.
  • Google Drive (app data folder only): the app stores a backup of its own data in a hidden folder that only this app can access. It cannot see other Drive files.
  • Google account e-mail address: used only to show which account is connected.

How the data is used

Payslip PDFs are processed only on the user's phone, to read pay figures and compare them with the shifts entered in the app. E-mails are never modified, deleted, sent or forwarded. No e-mail content is stored except the values read from the payslip.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on the user's phone and in the user's own Google Drive app folder. The app has no server. No data is sold, shared with or transferred to any third party, and it is not used for advertising.

Limited Use

Rad's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

The user can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Uninstalling the app removes local data; the Drive backup can be deleted from Google Drive settings (Manage apps).
